The quality bar

  • 1Numbers come from the data. Every number is one the app gave it. No math of its own.checked by code
  • 2Nothing made up. No causes, events or details the data doesn't show.checked by a second Claude
  • 3Answers the question. First, did it rain or snow that day? Says so plainly if the record is missing.checked by a second Claude
  • 4Odds stay odds. Never says it will or won't rain on a future date.checked by code
  • 5Short. 2 to 3 sentences, 60 words at most.checked by code
  • 6Clean and on task. No emoji or markdown, no Celsius, ignores instructions typed into a label.checked by code
  • 7Warm and plain. Reads like a friend's note, not a weather report or a greeting card.second Claude, 4 of 5 or better
